Main Service

E-Commerce Account Handling

We take over the daily operation of your seller account on eBay, Walmart, Etsy, Amazon and other platforms we have hands-on experience with. You keep the vision and the business; we keep the store running.

Each account gets a defined scope, a clear point of contact and regular reporting.

  • Listing creation, optimization and periodic audits
  • Titles, descriptions, keywords and product attributes
  • Inventory and price monitoring
  • Order management and fulfillment coordination
  • Customer messages, returns and refunds handling
  • Account health, performance metrics and policy compliance
  • Scheduled performance reports and recommendations

Account Handling On Percentage

Percentage-Based Management

For eligible stores, we manage your account in return for an agreed percentage of sales, profit or another clearly defined performance metric, so our results are tied to yours.

Not every store qualifies. We review your account, products and margins before agreeing to this model.

Everything is written down first. Before we begin we define whether the percentage is calculated on gross sales, net sales or profit, plus how expenses, refunds and chargebacks are treated, payment timing, account ownership and access, reporting, and termination terms.
  • Fee basis defined: gross sales, net sales or profit
  • Treatment of refunds, chargebacks and expenses
  • Payment schedule and reporting cadence
  • Account ownership and access terms
  • Clear termination terms

Order Processing

  • Order review and processing across marketplaces

  • Shipping coordination and tracking updates
  • Delivery monitoring and issue follow-up
  • Returns and refund support
  • Available as a stand-alone service or part of account handling

Business Setup

LLC & LTD Formation Assistance

Selling seriously on marketplaces often starts with a proper business entity. We guide you through the process and coordinate the paperwork.

This is formation assistance and administrative coordination. We are not a law or tax firm and do not provide regulated legal or tax advice; where needed we introduce you to qualified professionals.
  • Guidance on what LLC and LTD registration involves
  • Documentation checklist and coordination
  • Registration support
  • Introductions to relevant professional providers where needed
  • Setup help so your business details match your marketplace accounts
